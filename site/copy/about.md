## 1. Why Sthara exists (first person, signed)
H2: Ten schools. No record that followed me.
Body:
I changed schools ten times growing up. My parent's job meant a transfer every few years, and every move landed in the middle of a syllabus. The new school was always a little ahead or a little behind, and nobody there knew what I had already covered.

So the gaps stayed hidden. A topic I had missed in one school was assumed in the next. By the time a report card showed it, the gap had grown into something much harder to close. No teacher was careless. Each school simply started from nothing, because my record never came with me.

Sthara is the system I wished had existed: one record that keeps the whole picture of a student, so a slipping topic shows up the week it slips, and nothing a child has learned or missed gets lost between classrooms.

Signature: Moses Benhur, Co-founder, Managing Director & CFO

Callout: What we do, in one line. We replace the register, the spreadsheet, the WhatsApp group and the tutoring app with one living record that every role in the school reads from.

## 2. The problem we started with
H2: We didn't start with AI. We started with a problem.
Body: Schools produce a great deal of information every day: attendance, homework, tests, teacher observations, messages from home. Most of it lives in different places. A school can know what a student scored without knowing what the student understands, and by the time a gap appears on a report card, a term may already be gone.
Body: Technology should not add one more place to look. It should bring what the school already knows into one view, help teachers decide, give leaders a clear picture and get students help while it still matters.

## 3. Built close to schools (trimmed)
H2: Built close to schools.
Body: Sthara is founder-led. The best school software is built by people who stay close to the students, teachers, leaders and parents who use it every day. We listen to what works, find where the real friction is and keep improving. We don't tell schools what they need; we find out, and build for it.

## 5. What we believe
H2: Four things we won't trade away.
- One record, never four. Every part of Sthara writes to the same record. We won't build anything that creates a second version of the truth about a child.
- Teacher time is sacred. AI earns its place by giving teachers hours back: marking, lesson preparation, report writing. If a feature adds a step for a teacher, it doesn't ship.
- The office runs on facts, not follow-ups. Fees, attendance and staff records update from the work already done, so the office stops chasing paper and re-typing what someone already entered.
- Children's data is children's data. Role-based access, audit trails, servers and student records in India, and no advertising to children, ever. Link: See our DPDP approach.

## 6. How we work (philosophy, trimmed)
H2: Stay close. Solve real problems. Keep people at the centre.
- Stay close to schools. We keep listening to school leaders, teachers, students and parents.
- Solve real problems. We don't build something because it is possible. We build it when it fixes something that matters.
- Keep people at the centre. AI should support a teacher's judgement, not replace it, and help a student think, not think for them.
Line: Education is too important to build from a distance.
